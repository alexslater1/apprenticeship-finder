# UK employers with data-related apprenticeships — seed list

Compiled 2026-10-09 for a Year 13 school leaver targeting a **September 2027** start. Scope: L4 Data Analyst, L6 Data Scientist (integrated degree), L6 Digital & Technology Solutions (DTS) with a data pathway, L5 Data Engineer, L6/L7 AI / Machine Learning. L3 Data Technician is noted where it is an employer's only data route.

**145 employers in the seed** (`employers.seed.json`, same order). 87 more were checked and left out (see the last two sections and `employers.excluded.json`).

**Columns.** *Relevance*: **core** means a named data, AI or ML scheme, or a DTS data pathway. **adjacent** means a tech or DTS degree apprenticeship whose data pathway is not named, an L3-only data route, or a data scheme mainly for existing staff. *Confidence*: **verified** means the data-relevant scheme was seen in 2024–26 on the employer's site, on gov.uk Find an Apprenticeship, or on a credible source such as Higherin/RateMyApprenticeship or a university page. **likely** means strong but indirect or partial evidence. **unverified** means it was inferred. *ATS* shows the vendor plus the host seen in apply links. The JSON `ats_family` field gives the back-end platform to scrape, ignoring marketing front-ends such as Radancy or Phenom.

**Caveats.** Application windows come from past cycles or current pages and may close early once roles are filled. Treat live status from the daily checker as authoritative. A few career sites (Civil Service Jobs, MI5/MI6, Home Office, Fujitsu, Bloomberg, Sage, Centrica, ScottishPower, Anglian Water, UKPN, Aviva, Allianz) block bots or need JavaScript, so expect to need a headless browser or an alternative feed such as Find an Apprenticeship.
